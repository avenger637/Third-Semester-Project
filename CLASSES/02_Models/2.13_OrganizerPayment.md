# 2.13 OrganizerPayment.java

Represents a payment or salary disbursement to an organizer.

## Fields
- `int paymentId` — unique payment identifier.
- `int organizerId` — organizer receiving payment.
- `BigDecimal amount` — payment amount.
- `LocalDateTime paymentDate` — date/time of payment.
- `PaymentStatus status` — payment state.

## Constructor
`OrganizerPayment(int paymentId, int organizerId, BigDecimal amount)`

## Methods
- `getPaymentId()`
- `getOrganizerId()`
- `getAmount()`
- `getPaymentDate()`
- `getStatus()`
- `setStatus(PaymentStatus status)`

## Notes
Use `BigDecimal` for money. Persist payment records; do not treat a status change as proof that money was actually transferred.
